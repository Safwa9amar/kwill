HowRetyped by the operator from the student's draft; logo pasted from the web; title must match the approved title word for word.

Kwill: reads the fields from the old cover (or the file), student confirms in one tap, then their own cover cleaned or the university standard.

Deposit packالإيداع · Dépôt

v1 · proposed
3.5–7 DA / printed page · binding per copy · CD
HowPrint 2–3 bound copies + a CD at a shop, or upload to an online printer (Yalidine delivery 24–72 h, cash on delivery). Files: PDF + Word, a signed integrity declaration, the supervisor's deposit authorisation, an abstract sheet.
PainDeposit is moving to PDF-only on the supervisor's platform account (Khenchela 2025-26) — the PDF must be right the first time; queues in June.
Kwill: the checked PDF + .docx with fonts embedded, the abstract sheet, and the two forms prefilled. Printing stays at any shop — or a partner later.
Univ. Khenchela 2025-26 notice; Constantine 2; Ghardaïa; Sétif 2; print-pdf.dz.

Similarityنسبة التشابه · Plagiat

later · honest
Bundled with formatting · "mise en page et plagiat"
HowShops "reduce the percentage" by rewording until the checker passes. Faculties gate the defense on it — e.g. ≤ 40% at Khenchela. Arrêté 1082 (2020) sanctions plagiarism, up to withdrawing the degree.
PainRewording-to-evade is a risk the student carries alone.
Kwill: a pre-check that finds unquoted borrowed passages and cites them — never a rewriter. Own spec, third-party bill.
Arrêté n° 1082 du 27/12/2020; Khenchela deposit notice.

Writing it for themإنجاز المذكرة

never
50 000 – 200 000 DA · per press reports
HowHalf paid upfront, topic handed to "specialists". The professors' union called for an investigation and for annulling such theses.
PainFraud; degree can be withdrawn.
Kwill: never. The universities are the channel — Rescue only touches presentation, never authorship.
Echorouk; El Raed (09/06/2019).
The shop's real product is "it opens right". Listings sell "anti-décalage" — the file not shifting on the print shop's PC. Embedding the fonts and delivering a checked PDF answers that by construction.
Revisions are where shops earn twice. The supervisor changes chapter 2, the TOC and numbering break, the student pays again. A re-run that costs nothing is the one thing a shop can't match.
New formats are coming. The 2025 amendment to arrêté 1275 adds the business-model mémoire ("مشروع مؤسسة اقتصادية"); the ministry targets 80% of final-year students. Faculties also move to IMRAD. Rescue must recognise the format, not assume one.

Decide

Four calls the research raised. Click one option per question, then tell me in the terminal (or just say "recommended").

1 · What is in the first version?
The shop's deliverable is a file that is ready to deposit, not just formatted pages.
A

Rescue + cover + deposit packrecommended

Everything in the flow above: formatted .docx and checked PDF (fonts embedded), abstract sheet, integrity declaration + deposit authorisation prefilled.

B

Rescue + cover only

The .docx and PDF. No forms, no abstract sheet. Smaller, ships sooner.

C

A + printing & delivery partner at launch

One tap to print and bind copies, delivered to the wilaya. Needs a partner agreement; printing is the one thing a shop still does.

2 · What happens after the supervisor's changes?
This is where shops get paid twice — and the clearest reason to choose Kwill over them.
A

Free re-runs on the same thesis until the defenserecommended

One purchase covers every revision of that thesis. Cost is bounded: the same file, mostly cached pages.

B

Two re-runs included

Then each further run is paid. Easier to price, weaker message.

C

Every run is paid

Same model as the shop.

3 · How do we beat "décalage"?
Shops advertise it because files shift on another PC. Arabic fonts are the usual culprit.
A

Embed fonts in the .docx and deliver the checked PDFrecommended

Word honours embedded fonts on any PC. First task in the plan: prove the engine can embed them, and check the font licences.

B

PDF is the deposit file; the .docx is "as is"

No engine work; the .docx may still shift on another PC.

4 · Which shop service do we replace next?
Each is its own spec after Rescue. The season decides the order: field work Feb–Apr, deposit May, defense June.
A

By season: SPSS field study → similarity pre-check → defense packrecommended

Each one lands just before students need it.

B

Defense pack first

The most emotional moment; widest appeal, but only useful in June.

C

Similarity pre-check first

The scariest gate for students; needs a third-party checker and its bill.
