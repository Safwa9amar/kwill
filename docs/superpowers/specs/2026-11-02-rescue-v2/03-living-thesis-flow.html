Honest failure
If Prove fails: no broken file, a refund, the original untouched, and the case goes to Support. It's rare, but it decides whether students trust the product.

Motion: what makes it smooth

  • One screen. The top half stays; only the sheet changes height and content, on a spring.
  • Before → after is a sweep cross-fade on the page, not a screen change.
  • Pages fill in from skeletons; the step bar fills; numbers count up. Nothing jumps.
  • A light haptic on every answer and on "ready".

Comfort: what keeps it calm

  • Copy says "we'll fix", never "errors" or "violations".
  • "Your words won't change" and "the original is kept" are repeated at the moments of doubt: upload, payment, result.
  • Every question has "let Kwill decide". Nobody is blocked.
  • Safe to leave at any point. A push brings them back to the right screen.

How it fits the app

  • App tokens only (useThemeColors, brand orange, slate); light and dark; RTL-first, with fr/en mirrored.
  • The sheet is a gorhom bottom sheet; page images come from Kwill's converter.
  • One route, /rescue, driven by the saved run's state, so a cold start resumes on the right step.
  • The result thesis opens in the normal workspace.

The student's note: what it can and can't do

  • Can: formatting wishes ("French quotes in Times 12"), structure hints ("chapter 3 is the applied part"), exceptions ("no number on the dedication"), "keep my tables as they are".
  • Asked, not obeyed blindly: anything that clashes with the department guide. Both values are shown and the student picks.
  • Can't: change their words, skip the final checks, or break the deposit rules. Those are enforced in code, whatever the note says.
  • Read by the model at Map and Review; parsed into rules where possible; saved in the rescue run for every re-run.
